Meet the Founder

Lisa Haven

Founder & Chief Executive Officer · HAVENLY · VIXA · LUMAPROMPT.AI

Lisa Haven is a serial entrepreneur and beauty visionary based in Florida, building brands at the intersection of innovation, self-care, and Gen Z culture. As the founder of Havenly, she set out to reimagine the everyday makeup remover wipe — creating something bigger, thicker, and actually worthy of your skin. Dermatologist tested, female founded, and made in the United States of America, Havenly is her love letter to every woman who deserves better.

A devoted mother of three daughters — Valentina, Mili, and Scarlett — and proud dog mom to Lucky, Lisa is also the founder of LumaPrompt.ai and VIXA. Her mission: build world-class brands that empower women to show up as their most confident, unfiltered selves.
