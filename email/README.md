# The Haven: new post email

Every lisahaven.co post goes to The Haven subscribers (Beehiiv publication "The Haven",
pub_9588951e-726c-4499-a402-8b7a425b766f) the same day it publishes.
Lisa approved this design on Nov 4, 2026. Do not change the layout, images or order without asking her.

Look: `approved-preview.html` (open it in a browser). Images live in `/images/email/`:
- `the-haven-email-header.jpg`: "Lisa Haven" wordmark with black heart, small circle photo, "The insider letter.", gradient band.
- `the-haven-email-signoff.jpg`: small circle photo, "Talk soon, Lisa".

Beehiiv is on the Free plan, so its MCP is read-only. Build each email in the Beehiiv editor
in Lisa's Chrome (app.beehiiv.com > New post), in this order:

1. Image: https://lisahaven.co/images/email/the-haven-email-header.jpg (links to https://lisahaven.co/)
2. "Hi friend,"
3. 2 short intro paragraphs in Lisa's voice, taken from the post's opening (never invent her stories or numbers)
4. Divider
5. Small label: NEW ON THE BLOG
6. Image: the post cover https://lisahaven.co<image from frontmatter>, linked to the post
7. Heading: the post title, with 1 key phrase in italic
8. 1 sentence summary (from the excerpt)
9. Bulleted list, 4 points, from the post's main subheadings
10. Button: "Read the full post →" to https://lisahaven.co/blog/<slug>/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=<slug>
11. Divider
12. Quote block: the post's closing quote, author in the caption (centered style)
13. Divider
14. "Your turn:" a question tied to the post, then "Hit reply and tell me. I read every email."
15. Image: https://lisahaven.co/images/email/the-haven-email-signoff.jpg
16. P.S. about The Haven Playbook series with a link to https://lisahaven.co/shop
17. Links: Blog · Instagram · LinkedIn · Pinterest, then VIXA · HAVENLY · LUMAPROMPT.AI

Subject line: short and curious, under 50 characters. Preview text: 1 line from the post.
Audience: all free subscribers. Theme: cream background #F2EDE6, ink #0A0A0A, dusty rose #C47E8A.

Rules: no em dashes, numbers as numerals, "mom" or "mother of 3" only, never mention her ex,
family or legal matters. Send a test to hello@lisahaven.co first and look at it.
Do not send to subscribers until the email footer has a real mailing address (Beehiiv
Settings > Publication > Emails). Never use Lisa's home address and never make one up.
