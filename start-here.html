New here? Welcome to The Haven.

I'm Lisa Haven — a 40-year-old founder, single mother of three daughters, and serial entrepreneur based in Florida. I'm building three companies at once, and this blog is where I share all of it: the wins, the mess, the fashion, the mindset, and the real behind-the-scenes of building as a solo parent. No filter. No apologies.
