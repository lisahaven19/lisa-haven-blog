Short bio

Lisa Haven is a Florida-based founder and single mother of three building three companies at the intersection of technology, beauty, and women's empowerment: HAVENLY (Gen Z makeup remover wipes headed for Sephora shelves), VIXA (an artificial intelligence dating clarity app for women), and LUMAPROMPT.AI (a human-written prompt library built for women).

Long bio

Lisa Haven discovered her first passion — photography — the moment her first daughter was born, and spent eight years behind the lens before moving into consumer packaged goods beauty and then mobile apps. Today she is a 40-year-old serial entrepreneur and single mother of three daughters, building three brands at once from Florida: HAVENLY, a bigger, thicker, dermatologist-tested makeup remover wipe designed for the Gen Z market and made in the United States of America; VIXA, a next-generation dating clarity app whose artificial intelligence companion turns messy conversations into sharp, private, emotionally neutral insight; and LUMAPROMPT.AI, a premium prompt library authored by human industry expert writers for women who expect more. She uses artificial intelligence daily across all of her businesses and shares her journey — wins, mess, and everything in between — on her blog at lisahaven.co.
