## Why your ChatGPT answers feel generic

If you have ever searched "ChatGPT ask question" because your answers keep missing the mark, you are in the right place. Here is the truth: ChatGPT answers what you give it. Nothing more.

When you type "write me an Instagram caption," you are asking it to guess your brand, your audience, your tone and your goal all at once. It cannot read your mind, so it averages everything out and hands you something that sounds like everybody else.

Vague question in, vague answer out. Specific question in, specific answer out.

Even OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, says the same thing in its own [prompting best practices guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10032626-prompt-engineering-best-practices-for-chatgpt): be clear and specific, give it a role, and add context. The good news is you do not need to be technical to do any of this. You just need to ask like you are talking to a smart new assistant on her first day.

## How to ask ChatGPT a question in 5 simple steps

Every great question I ask has the same 5 parts. Learn them once and you can use them in any AI tool.

![How to ask ChatGPT a question: adding 1 sentence, "Let's think step by step," raised a model's score on math word problems from 17.7% to 78.7% in a 2022 study. The 5 steps: tell it who to be, give your real situation, say what you want back, set your limits, let it ask you first.](/images/uploads/how-to-ask-chatgpt-a-question-chart.jpg)

### Step 1: Tell it who to be

Start with a role. "Act as a retail buyer for a national beauty chain." "Act as a patient math tutor." "Act as a marketing expert who has launched apps for women."

A role tells ChatGPT which kind of knowledge to pull from and how to talk to you. It is the fastest upgrade there is.

### Step 2: Give it your real situation

This is where most people stop short. Tell it who you are, who you are helping, and what you are trying to do. Use real numbers. "I am a founder ordering my first 10,000 units" is worth more than any fancy wording.

### Step 3: Say exactly what you want back

A list? A table? 3 options? A script you can read out loud? An email under 100 words? Say it. If you do not tell ChatGPT the format, it picks one for you, and it is usually a long wall of text.

### Step 4: Set your limits

Tone, length, and anything to avoid. "Warm but direct." "No jargon." "Do not suggest paid tools." Limits are what make the answer sound like it was made for you.

### Step 5: Let it ask you questions first

This is the move almost nobody uses, and it is my favorite. End your question with: "Before you answer, ask me any questions you need."

Instead of guessing, ChatGPT will ask you 3 or 4 smart questions. You answer them, and the final answer is built on your real life instead of an average one.

Here is the full formula in 1 prompt. Copy it, fill in the brackets, and save it somewhere you can find it.

```prompt
Act as [the expert you need].
Here is my situation: [who you are, who you are helping, your goal, your real numbers].
I want: [exactly what you want back: a list, a table, 3 options, a script, an email].
Keep it [your tone], under [length], and do not [anything to avoid].
Before you answer, ask me any questions you need to give me the best answer.
```

**Here is something surprising.** In a 2022 study by researchers from the University of Tokyo and Google, adding 1 short sentence to the end of a question, "Let's think step by step," raised an AI model's score on a set of math word problems from 17.7% to 78.7% ([read the study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11916)). Today's ChatGPT is much smarter and often reasons on its own. But the lesson still holds: how you ask changes what you get.

## Before and after: how I ask ChatGPT questions for my 3 companies

These are real questions from my real work week. Look at the before, then copy the after.

### Havenly: talking to a manufacturer

**Before:** "How do I negotiate with a manufacturer?"

**After:**

```prompt
Act as a sourcing expert who has negotiated with beauty manufacturers for 20 years.
I am a founder ordering my first 10,000 units of a makeup remover wipe for Gen Z.
Give me the 5 leverage points I have as a first time buyer, and the exact words to use for each one.
Keep it friendly and confident. No threats, no bluffing.
```

The first version gets you a general article. The second gets you a script you can use on your next call.

### VIXA: planning a launch

**Before:** "Give me marketing ideas for my app."

**After:**

```prompt
Act as a growth marketer who has launched consumer apps for women.
My app helps women get clarity on confusing dating conversations. My audience is women ages 22 to 40 who live on TikTok and Instagram.
Give me 5 launch ideas ranked from lowest to highest cost, with the very first step for each one.
Show it as a table.
```

**After:**

I post 5 times a week about how I use AI to run my businesses as a mom. My blog is new and I am not paying for SEO tools yet.
Give me 10 proven ways to get more readers in the next 90 days, ranked by how much time each one takes.

That is the exact kind of question that got me my idea about writing AI case studies for other companies. A real goal and real limits got me a real plan.

## How I ask AI now: I just talk to it

Here is my honest confession. These days I barely type my questions at all.

I run 3 companies and raise 3 daughters, so I move fast. I jump from window to window and I just talk to AI out loud, like I would talk to a business partner. Both ChatGPT and Claude have voice options, and talking is often faster than typing.

But even when I talk fast, I still use the 5 steps. They are built into how I speak. My go to move is 1 word: **assess.**

**How I used to ask:** "What does this mean?"

**How I ask now:**

```prompt
Assess this conversation and these screenshots.
Tell me what is really going on, what I might be missing, and give me 2 ways to reply.
I will make the final call.
```

That 1 word does 3 things:

1. **It asks for judgment, not just facts.** "Assess" tells AI to think like an advisor, not a search engine.
2. **It asks for options.** 2 replies to choose from, not 1 answer to accept.
3. **It keeps me in charge.** AI gives me the read. I make the decision, and I make it faster.

I use this every day for creator questions, customer messages, business choices and even my own texts. AI does not make my decisions for me. It helps me make better ones, quicker. ## What is the best way to ask ChatGPT questions after the first answer?

The first answer is a first draft. The magic is in the follow up. ChatGPT remembers everything you said earlier in the same chat, so treat it like a conversation, not a vending machine.

Here are the 6 follow ups I use most:

- **"Make it shorter."** Cuts the fluff fast.
- **"Make it sound more like me. Here is something I wrote:"** Then paste a few lines of your own writing.
- **"What am I missing?"** It will point out gaps you did not ask about.
- **"Give me 3 other options."** Great when the first idea is close but not quite right.
- **"Explain it like I am 12."** Perfect for anything technical.
- **"Turn this into a checklist."** My favorite for anything I need to actually do.

## How to ask AI better questions in any tool

These steps are not just for ChatGPT. They work in Claude, Gemini and any other AI chat tool, because every one of them answers what you give it. Keep this cheat sheet handy.

| Step | Ask yourself | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Who to be | Which expert do I need? | "Act as a patient tutor" |
| 2. Your situation | What would a new assistant need to know? | "I have 3 kids and 20 minutes" |
| 3. What you want back | What shape should the answer take? | "A table with 3 options" |
| 4. Your limits | What tone and length? What to avoid? | "Under 100 words, no jargon" |
| 5. Let it ask first | What might it need to know that I forgot? | "Ask me any questions first" |

1 more tip: keep your best questions. When a question gets you a great answer, save it in your notes. Next time, you only change the brackets.

## FAQ

**Do I need to write long questions for ChatGPT?**
Not long, just specific. 2 to 5 clear sentences with a role, your situation, the format you want and your limits will beat a vague 1 liner every time.

**How do I ask ChatGPT a quick question without messing up my main chat?**
Open a new chat for anything off topic, or use a temporary chat, which does not save to your history. Keep 1 chat per project so ChatGPT stays focused on that work.
