I Built 3 Businesses and a Full AI App Using Only Two Tools. Here Is Exactly How.
By Lisa Haven  |  lisahaven.co
Female Founder · AI for Women in Business · Build with AI · Women in Tech · AI App Builder · Entrepreneurship
In 2 weeks I built a full AI app from scratch.
On Easter day I sat at my computer for 13 hours straight and built the entire infrastructure of VIXA, an AI dating clarity app, with zero technical background and no development team.
I also run a Gen Z beauty brand called HAVENLY, a premium AI prompt platform called LUMAPROMPT.AI, and this blog where I document everything. Four brands. One founder. And right now, only two tools powering all of it.
I am going to tell you exactly what those tools are. But first, let me show you what I am building because the context matters.
What I'm Building
VIXA — The AI Dating Clarity App for Women
VIXA is not a dating app. It is the clarity layer on top of dating. Women paste a conversation from Hinge, Bumble, or their texts and our AI companion Vela reads the tone, timing, gaps, and effort level to tell you exactly what is actually happening. No more midnight spirals. No more group chat guesswork. Just real answers, privately and instantly.
Early access is live now on iPhone at VIXAapp.ai. 10 free analyses per week. No credit card required.
HAVENLY — The Gen Z Beauty Brand Built for Sephora
HAVENLY is a makeup remover wipe reimagined for Gen Z. Bigger, thicker, and built differently than anything currently on the market. The goal is global distribution with a clear path to Sephora shelves.
Follow the brand at Havenly.co.
LUMAPROMPT.AI — Premium AI Prompts Written for Women
LUMAPROMPT.AI is a premium prompt library written by human industry experts, built specifically for women who want better results from AI. Not machine-generated. Written by real professionals who understand real-world business outcomes. Better prompts. Better results. Less guesswork.
Explore it at Lumaprompt.ai.
LISAHAVEN.CO — The Blog Where I Document Everything
This blog. Where I share every tool, every strategy, and every lesson from building four brands using AI. Built for women who want to do the same thing.
Now Here Is the Part That Makes This Even More Interesting
I am 40 years old. I am a single mom of three daughters. I share custody, which means my schedule looks nothing like a traditional founder's schedule.
When I do not have my kids, I work 13 hours a day. When I do have them, I work 5 hours in the morning while my sitter is here. And honestly, what would I do without her.
I do not have a computer science degree. I am not a developer. I did not come from money or connections in tech.
What I have is an obsession with AI, an idea I could not let go of, and a deep passion for building things that actually matter. I work the hours I do because I genuinely love this. Not because I have to. Because I am hungry for it.
And right now I am doing all of it with just two tools until I can start bringing in contractors to help run operations.
How I Found the Tools That Changed Everything
When I launched LUMAPROMPT.AI I hired a web developer because I did not know there was another way. Most people start there. It costs more, takes longer, and you are always dependent on someone else to make changes.
Then I came across a post from an app founder who had built their entire product using Lovable, an AI-powered app builder. Something clicked instantly. If a non-developer could build a fully functioning app with AI, what else was possible?
I opened Claude and started brainstorming. What it designed for me was so beautiful I could not stop. The layout. The structure. The flow. Better than what I had paid a developer to produce.
So I kept going. I designed VIXA with Claude. HAVENLY with Claude. This blog with Claude. And on Easter day I used Claude Code to build the entire technical infrastructure of VIXA in 13 hours.
It has been the best business decision I have ever made.
The Only Two Tools Powering My Entire Business Right Now
I want to be completely transparent. Right now, my entire stack runs on two tools from Anthropic. That is it. No complex tech setup. No expensive software subscriptions. Just these two.
Claude Code — Build Real Software Without Knowing How to Code
Claude Code is an AI-powered development tool that lets non-developers build real, functioning software. You describe what you want to build. Claude Code writes the code, builds the infrastructure, and executes it. It is how I built the entire foundation of VIXA with no developer, no co-founder, and no prior experience.
If you have an app idea and you think you cannot build it because you do not know how to code, Claude Code changes that completely.
Claude Cowork — The AI Business Partner I Use Every Single Day
Claude Cowork is the desktop tool I use for everything that is not coding. Marketing strategy. Content creation. Brand design. Campaign planning. Competitive research. Writing. Brainstorming. It is like having the most brilliant business partner sitting next to you at all times, available 24 hours a day, who never gets tired and always comes ready.
This blog post was written with Claude Cowork. My VIXA marketing playbook was built with Claude Cowork. Every piece of content across all four brands starts here.
I genuinely cannot live without it.
And yes, I will be adding more AI tools to my stack as I grow. But I want you to know that everything I have built so far was done with just these two. Because I want you to see what is actually possible before you think you need anything more complicated.
