I built three companies before a single one made me a dollar. And instead of hiding until the money shows up, I am building in public before revenue, on purpose.



Most founders do the opposite. They stay quiet, work in private, and only start talking once the numbers look good and the story is safe. By then the hard part is invisible. You see the highlight reel, never the build.



I want you to see the build.



\## Why build in public before revenue?



Because self belief is the one asset that never runs out. Money can run out. Momentum can stall. But the belief that I will figure it out is something I control completely, and I refuse to wait for revenue to give me permission to back myself.



I am documenting all three companies in real time: \[VIXA](https://www.vixaapp.ai), my dating clarity app for women; \[HAVENLY](https://www.havenly.co), my Gen Z makeup remover wipe brand; and \[LUMAPROMPT.AI](https://www.lumaprompt.ai), my human written prompt library for women. None of them have to be finished for the journey to be worth sharing.



\## Why document the failures and not just the wins?



Because the failures are the part nobody shows, and they are the part that actually teaches. I have launched a business before that did not work. I am not ashamed of it and I am not embarrassed by it. Failing is part of the process, and I am not afraid of it.



The internet is full of after the fact success stories with the messy middle cut out. I would rather give you the honest version. The good, the hard, the ugly, and eventually the amazing outcome. Wins and corrections included.



\## What does failing forward actually mean?



It means every stumble and every study session is tuition. Every single day I watch a founder story or a marketing video and I come out sharper. A better founder. A better marketer. Even on a day that looks like nothing happened, I added knowledge I did not have yesterday.



So if one attempt falls short, I have not lost. I have gathered everything I need for the attempt that finally works. You are never standing still as long as you are still learning.



\## The part most people will not admit



I love this. I love the work. I love sitting at my computer, sometimes running my laptop and my desktop at the same time on two different tasks, and I am genuinely happy doing it.



Of course I want the outcome. I want to build real wealth and a legacy for my three daughters. But I stopped letting the outcome be the only thing that matters, because when you love the process this much, you have already won the part most people are still chasing.



\## The faith underneath all of it



Here is what I believe. Put your whole journey into your daily habits and success becomes a matter of time, not luck. The harder you work, the more you learn. The more you learn, the more doors open. And the more doors open, the closer you get, as long as you do not quit and you do not let one failure write the ending.



That is the faith I have. Not blind hope. Earned conviction, reinforced every day by showing up.



So I will keep building in public before the revenue. I will keep documenting the parts other founders hide. And I will keep believing, out loud, that something is going to work, because it is.



If you are a woman building something of your own, take this with you. You do not have to wait until you have made it to start. Start now. Be seen trying. Invest in yourself now. 💸



\> "Both faith and fear ask you to believe in something you cannot see. \*You choose.\*" 🖤
