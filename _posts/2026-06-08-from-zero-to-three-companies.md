Everyone sees the [vision board](https://lisahaven.co/blog/i-manifested-three-companies-heres-the-part-the-law-of-attraction-crowd-wont-tell-you/). Nobody sees the 3am work while the baby sleeps and the rest of the house has finally gone quiet.

I'm Lisa Haven, founder of **HAVENLY** ([www.havenly.co](https://www.havenly.co)), the makeup remover wipes brand built for Gen Z; **LUMAPROMPT.AI** ([www.lumaprompt.ai](https://www.lumaprompt.ai)), a premium prompt generator written for the way women actually think; and **VIXA** ([www.vixaapp.ai](https://www.vixaapp.ai)), the dating clarity app that hands women their power back. That's 3 companies and 3 daughters, and one woman in Florida building toward something that would make my girls proud.

**The Lie They Sell You**

They sell entrepreneurship like a highlight reel. You wake up one day with a brilliant idea, you pitch it, and *voilà*, you're on a Forbes list.

The real version looks different. You're prepping carnivore meals on Sunday because there's no time to cook during the week. You're answering developer messages with one hand while feeding a 15 month old with the other. And you carry all of it (the household, the companies, the vision) because that's exactly what you were built to do.

You do it anyway. That's really all there is to it.

**The Real Struggle Nobody Talks About: Focus**

This is the part the founder podcasts skip. Strategy and capital are hard, but the hardest thing about building 3 companies while raising 3 daughters is focus. I mean finding real, uninterrupted thinking time when your days belong to everyone but you.

3 kids, school runs, meals, laundry, and a toddler who doesn't care that you have a paywall architecture to finalize. Every hour of my day is claimed before it even starts.

So I built the one window that's entirely mine: 3am.

When the house is silent and Lucky is asleep at my feet (he's the only one who keeps me company at that hour), that's when I work. I'm not romanticizing the grind. That hour is simply the one that belongs to me. It's when I do the deep work, like product decisions, brand strategy and the thinking that actually moves a company forward. It's also when I learn. I study business operations courses. I study marketing. I watch founder stories until the sun comes up and pull lessons from people who built empires with less than I have.

I'm not waiting for someone to teach me. I'm teaching myself, one quiet morning at a time.

**Why 3?**

People ask me why I started 3 companies at once. The honest answer is that each one solves a problem I lived.

**HAVENLY** came from my obsession with skincare. I noticed nobody had touched the wipe market for Gen Z, the generation that actually reads ingredient lists and cares about how things look.

**LUMAPROMPT.AI** came from my frustration with artificial intelligence (AI) tools, software that mimics human reasoning, that felt like they were built by men, for men. The outputs kept missing the nuance of how women actually think and communicate.

**VIXA** came from too many conversations with my girlfriends, brilliant and successful women who were completely lost in the mixed signals of modern dating. We deserve clarity, not confusion.

## What a normal day looks like with 3 kids and 3 companies

People always ask me what my day actually looks like. So here it is, the real version.

![A real day building 3 companies as a mom of 3: wake up before everyone at 5:30, morning brief from Claude, breakfast and school drop-off, deep work until 12, gym for 2 hours, school pickup and family time, laptop at night when needed.](/images/uploads/from-zero-to-three-companies-chart.jpg)

**5:30 to 6 AM: I wake up before anyone else.** In the very beginning, when I was first building my businesses, I woke up at 3 in the morning and worked for hours until it was done. Now it is closer to 5:30 or 6.

**Breakfast and my morning brief.** I eat, and I check my morning brief from Claude, my AI assistant. I set it up with my vision board, my to-do list and my goals. It tells me what needs fixing, what is not working and what matters today. It even reads my emails for me. I wrote exactly how to set this up in [how to create an AI agent with Claude](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-agent-with-claude/).

**Breakfast for my girls.** I make breakfast, everyone gets ready, and on the days my girls are with me, they head to school.

**Morning until 12: deep work.** My babysitter watches Scarlett until noon. This is when I get my most important work done.

**Gym for about 2 hours.** This is non-negotiable. Then I am done working for the day.

**Afternoon and evening: my girls.** I pick them up from school and spend time with them. Sometimes I make dinner. I know how to cook, so I keep it simple. Sometimes we go out to eat.

**Walks with Lucky.** My little dog gets his walks too. He has been with me through all of it.

**Weekends: family time.** Not always quiet, though. 1 of my daughters has soccer practice, so I take her.

## I don't stop when I'm tired. I stop when I'm done.

This is my rule, and it is the honest answer to "how do you do it all."

I stop when the work is done, when 1 of my kids needs me, or when it is time for school pickup or the gym. Not before.

On the days my girls are not with me, I work much longer hours. I plan my week so the big tasks land on those days. And if I do not finish something during the day, I spend a couple of hours on my laptop at night.

## How AI helps me run 3 companies as a mom

I always have my laptop with me. I have a desktop, my phone, my iPad and my laptop, and the laptop goes everywhere, including my car, in case something needs to get done.

But the real secret is AI. Claude helps me with almost everything:

- **My morning brief**, so I start every day knowing exactly what matters
- **Reminders**, set up for me so nothing slips
- **My creator program**, which is 1 of the most important parts of launching VIXA right now
- **Decisions**, because it helps me think through options fast and clearly

That is how 1 woman runs 3 companies. Not by doing more, but by letting AI carry the busywork so I can focus on the work only I can do. Here are [the AI tools I actually use](/blog/ai-tools-for-entrepreneurs/).

**What Keeps Me Going**

My foundation is simple. It's me, my faith, my 3 daughters, Valentina (14), Mili (12) and Scarlett (15 months), and Lucky, my dog, who has logged more 3am shifts beside me than anyone. I rely on myself, on God, and on the family I'm building all of this for.

My girls are watching everything: the discipline, the 3am alarm, the refusal to quit. I'm raising 3 future bosses, and every choice I make is a lesson they're soaking up. A woman builds her own life, on her own terms, and she doesn't ask anyone's permission to do it.

I'm the one I count on. That's been enough to build everything you see so far, and there's so much more coming.

We're just getting started. 💸

## The hardest moment of a normal day

The hardest part is doing it all with 3 kids. Some days everything lands on me at once: school, meals, homework, a toddler, and 3 companies.

And here is the honest part. Even when I am with my girls, my work is on my mind. I love what I do. I am obsessed with my goals and my businesses, so they never fully leave my head.

So I made a simple rule for myself: when I am with my kids, the phone and the computer go to the side. When I take my daughter to the park, I am at the park. Fully there.

The ideas will still be there when I get back. My girls' childhood will not wait.

## Building a business while raising kids FAQ

**How do you find time to build a business with kids?**
Protect 1 block of time every day that belongs only to the work. For me, that is the morning until 12.

**Do you work every day?**
Yes, but not the same way. I work shorter days when my girls are with me and longer days when they are not.

**What is the best tool for a busy mom running a business?**
An AI assistant set up to brief you every morning and handle reminders. It saves me hours every week.
