Everyone sees the vision board. Nobody sees the 3am work while the baby sleeps and the rest of the house has finally gone quiet.

I'm Lisa Haven — founder of **HAVENLY** ([www.havenly.co](https://www.havenly.co)), the makeup remover wipes brand built for Gen Z; **LUMAPROMPT.AI** ([www.lumaprompt.ai](https://www.lumaprompt.ai)), a premium prompt generator written for the way women actually think; and **VIXA** ([www.vixaapp.ai](https://www.vixaapp.ai)), the dating clarity app that hands women their power back. Three companies. Three daughters. One woman in Florida, building toward something that would make my girls proud.

**The Lie They Sell You**

They sell entrepreneurship like it's a highlight reel. One day you wake up, have a brilliant idea, pitch it, and *voilà* — you're on a Forbes list.

The real version? You're prepping carnivore meals on Sunday because there is no time to cook during the week. You're answering developer messages with one hand while feeding a 15-month-old with the other. And you carry all of it — the household, the companies, the vision — because that is exactly what you were built to do.

But you do it anyway. That's the whole thing.

**The Real Struggle Nobody Talks About: Focus**

Here is the part the founder podcasts skip. The hardest thing about building three companies while raising three daughters is not the strategy. It's not the capital. It's **focus** — finding uninterrupted, high-quality thinking time when your days belong to everyone but you.

Three kids. School runs. Meals. Laundry. A toddler who does not care that you have a paywall architecture to finalize. Every hour of my day is claimed before it begins.

So I built the one window that is entirely mine: **3am.**

When the house is silent — Lucky asleep at my feet, the only one who keeps me company at that hour — that is when I work. Not because I'm romanticizing the grind, but because that is the hour that belongs to me. That's when I do the deep work: the product decisions, the brand strategy, the thinking that actually moves a company forward. And it's when I learn. I study business operations courses. I study marketing. I watch founder stories until the sun comes up, pulling lessons from people who built empires from less than I have.

I'm not waiting to be taught. I'm teaching myself, one quiet morning at a time.

**Why Three?**

People ask why I started three companies at once. Honest answer: each one solves a problem I lived.

**HAVENLY** came from my obsession with skincare and noticing the wipe market hadn't been touched for Gen Z — the generation that actually reads ingredient lists and cares about aesthetics.

**LUMAPROMPT.AI** came from being frustrated with artificial intelligence tools — software that mimics human reasoning — that felt built by men, for men, producing outputs that missed the nuance of how women actually think and communicate.

**VIXA** came from too many conversations with girlfriends — brilliant, successful women — who were completely lost in the mixed signals of modern dating. We deserve clarity, not confusion.

**What Keeps Me Going**

My foundation is simple: me, my faith, my three daughters — Valentina (14), Mili (12), and Scarlett (15 months) — and Lucky, my dog, who's logged more 3am shifts beside me than anyone. I rely on myself, on God, and on the family I'm building this for.

My girls are watching everything. The discipline. The 3am alarm. The refusal to quit. I am raising three future bosses, and every choice I make is a lesson they're absorbing — that a woman builds her own life, on her own terms, and asks no one's permission to.

I am the one I count on. And that has been enough to build everything you see — with so much more still coming.

We're just getting started. 💸
