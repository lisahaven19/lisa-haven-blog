Everyone sees the vision board. Nobody sees the 2am spreadsheets while the baby sleeps on your chest.

I'm Lisa Haven — founder of HAVENLY (makeup remover wipes for Gen Z), LUMAPROMPT.AI (premium prompt generator for women), and VIXA (the dating clarity app that gives women their power back). Three companies. Three daughters. One very determined single mom living in Florida and building toward something that would make my girls proud.

## The Lie They Sell You

They sell entrepreneurship like it's a highlight reel. Like one day you wake up, have a brilliant idea, pitch it to investors, and voilà — you're on a Forbes list.

The real version? You're crying in your car after a rejected pitch, eating carnivore meals you prepped on Sunday because there's no time to cook during the week, and FaceTiming your mom at 11pm because she's the only one who still believes in you unconditionally.

**But you do it anyway. That's the whole thing.**

## Why Three?

People ask me why I started three companies at once. Honest answer: each one solves a problem I lived.

HAVENLY came from my obsession with skincare and noticing the wipe market hadn't been touched for Gen Z — the generation that actually cares about ingredients and aesthetic.

LUMAPROMPT came from being frustrated with AI tools that felt built by men, for men, producing outputs that missed the nuance of how women actually think and communicate.

VIXA came from too many conversations with girlfriends — brilliant, successful women — who were completely lost in the mixed signals of modern dating. We deserve clarity, not confusion.

## What Keeps Me Going

My daughters: Valentina (14), Mili (12), and Scarlett (15 months). They are watching everything. The late nights, the discipline, the refusal to quit. I am raising three future bosses, and every choice I make is a lesson they're absorbing.

That Rolls Royce isn't just a car. It's a symbol I'm building — proof that a single mom in Florida with a dream and relentless work ethic can create something extraordinary.

We're just getting started. 💸
