Everyone wants the vision board. Almost nobody wants the invoice that comes with it.

I believe in the law of attraction — the idea that what you focus on, you pull toward you. I've lived it. But I'm going to tell you the part that doesn't fit on a pastel quote graphic: **manifestation is a contract, and the payment is action.** Skip the payment and the vision stays a fantasy forever.

**The Lie They Sell You**

They sell it like a vending machine. Visualize the car, feel the feelings, repeat the affirmation, and the universe delivers it to your driveway while you sleep.

That's not law of attraction. That's a coping mechanism dressed up as a strategy. The women selling you that version are usually selling you something else right behind it.

Here's the real version: belief is the ignition, not the engine. It gets you to start. It does not drive the car.

**What the Law of Attraction Actually Is**

Strip away the mysticism and there's something real underneath. When you obsess over a specific outcome, your brain quietly reorganizes around it. You start noticing opportunities you used to walk past. You hear the one sentence in a podcast that unlocks a decision. You spot the gap in a market — like a makeup wipe nobody built for Gen Z, which became **HAVENLY** ([www.havenly.co](https://www.havenly.co)).

Clarity is a filter. When you decide exactly what you're building, the world starts sorting itself into "useful" and "noise." That's not magic. That's focus doing what focus does. But it only switches on when the vision is specific enough to be uncomfortable.

**Action Is the Rent You Pay on the Vision**

I did not affirm three companies into existence. I built them.

**VIXA** ([www.vixaapp.ai](https://www.vixaapp.ai)), the artificial intelligence dating clarity app — software that reads patterns and gives women real answers — exists because I sat with developers and architected it screen by screen. **LUMAPROMPT.AI** ([www.lumaprompt.ai](https://www.lumaprompt.ai)) exists because I wrote, refined, and shipped. Every "manifestation" in my life has a paper trail of 3am work behind it.

The universe does not reward the woman who wants it most. It rewards the woman who moves most in the direction of what she wants. Wanting is common. Movement is rare. That's the entire game.

**How I Actually Practice It**

My version of the law of attraction is disciplined, not delusional.

I get crystal clear on the vision — specific enough that I can see it. Then I protect my mind like it's an asset, because it is. I don't feed it doubt, drama, or people who flinch at my goals. I speak my outcomes out loud before they're real, because language aims the brain. And I prove my discipline in the small things first: the carnivore diet, the gym, the 3am alarm. If I can keep my word to myself about a workout, I can keep it about a company. Discipline in one room leaks into every other room in the house.

Belief sets the destination. Discipline is the car. Action is the gas. You need all three or you go nowhere with a really nice vision board.

**The Foundation Under All of It**

I'm not doing this on willpower alone. My foundation is simple: me, my faith, and my three daughters — Valentina (14), Mili (12), and Scarlett (19 months) — plus Lucky, who never misses a 3am shift. I rely on myself, on God, and on the family I'm building this for.

When the doubt comes — and it comes — I don't argue with it. I out-work it. Then I hand the rest to God and go to sleep so I can do it again tomorrow.

**The Vision I'm Calling In**

I know exactly what I'm building toward, down to the detail. The brand on Sephora shelves. The dating clarity app sold. Part-time in Miami, surrounded by people who elevate me. Three daughters who grow up knowing their mother decided to be extraordinary and then did the work to back it up.

I can already see it. And every morning at 3am, I pay the rent on it.

That's the law of attraction nobody monetizes — because it asks you to do something. We're just getting started. 💸
