You know how it goes. Your phone buzzes, you screenshot the text and send it to 4 group chats. Everyone has a different theory. 2 hours later you're more confused than when you started, and he's gone on with his evening, completely unbothered.

We've all done it. I've done it. And because I was already building companies, I kept asking myself why there wasn't a tool for this.

That's how VIXA was born.

## What VIXA Actually Does

VIXA is a dating clarity app built with AI, made specifically for women. You paste in a conversation or upload screenshots, and our AI companion Vela reads it. Nothing fluffy or vague, just something specific you can act on.

Vela reads:
- **Tone**: is he warm, distant or putting on a show?
- **Timing**: gaps between replies and how consistent he is
- **Word choice**: real effort or the bare minimum
- **Behavioral patterns**: breadcrumbing, love-bombing or genuine interest

She tells you what's really going on, scores the overall vibe, flags any red flags and suggests replies that still sound like you, not some script you copied from a Reddit thread.

## Why This Matters

Women feel things deeply, and in most areas of life that's a real strength. But when you're trying to figure out whether someone is wasting your time, your feelings can get in the way of what the facts are plainly showing you.

VIXA is the clarity your best friend would give you if she had no filter, a perfect memory and no judgment at all.

## What's Coming

I'm building this for the woman who is done wasting emotional energy. She deserves better information, and she deserves VIXA.
