It is late Friday night. The house is quiet, and I am sitting here with a thought that will not leave me alone. I am a little late on posting tonight, but this one matters, because this is truly how I think and how I feel.

There is a saying I keep coming back to: **how you do one thing is how you do everything.** It means the way you handle 1 part of your life shows up in all the other parts. Your patterns follow you. In business, in friendships, in love, in the gym. And the more I look at my own life, the more I see it is so true.

Here is what this idea taught me about effort, about letting things flow, and about trusting that what is meant for you will find you.

## Where "How You Do One Thing Is How You Do Everything" Comes From

When I first heard this line, I thought there had to be a study behind it. So I looked it up.

The truth is, nobody knows exactly who said it first. You will also hear it as "how you do anything is how you do everything." It is often linked to Zen teachings, and the author Cheri Huber wrote a whole book with that title. Life coach Martha Beck and business author T. Harv Eker both made it popular, and [Elephant Journal notes the true source has never been verified](https://www.elephantjournal.com/?p=1315799).

So it is not a study. It is something better: a pattern people keep noticing, generation after generation, because it keeps being true.

And I have noticed it everywhere. In people. In places. In myself.

## How I Do Business Is How I Do Everything (And That Is My Problem)

Let me be honest with you, because that is what this blog is for.

When I love something, I do not just like it. I get obsessed with it. I go all in. When I am building a business, I will work for hours and hours, long nights, until something works. I do not give up. That is the entrepreneur in me, and I am proud of her.

But here is what I had to admit: **I bring that same energy into my relationships.** And it does not always work.

In business, you are supposed to overdo it. You push. You chase. You follow up 5 times. You show up when nobody asked you to.

In relationships, that same push can be too much. Sometimes it scares people away. I try too hard. I put in the effort I would put into launching an app, and people are not apps. You cannot force them to work.

The only thing I should ever force in my life is my business. Everything else needs room to breathe.

So I am learning a new skill. It is not pushing. It is receiving. Sitting back and letting people show me who they are and what they want to give. For a woman who runs 3 companies, that is honestly harder than any launch.

### Where to push and where to let it flow

| | Business | Relationships |
|---|---|---|
| Effort | Overdo it. Always. | Match their energy. |
| When it is slow | Push harder and pivot | Step back and watch |
| When they go quiet | Follow up again | Let them come to you |
| What success looks like | Results you built | Effort that comes back to you |
| My rule | Never give up | Never beg anyone to stay |

## Never Beg Anyone to Stay

This is the heart of it.

You never want to beg someone to stay. Not in business, not in friendship, not in love. You never want to beg someone to do something for you, to show up for you, to be with you.

You want people to do what they naturally want to do. Because when you stop chasing, you finally get to see the truth. You see who shows up on their own.

That goes for business partners too. The best people I work with never needed convincing. The ones I had to chase usually cost me time, money, or peace.

Put in all you can. Then let it flow. Let life give back to you. When the energy comes back, match it, match the frequency, and keep going. That is the sign. That is how you know it is for you.

If something is for you, you do not have to question it. You do not have to wonder. You do not have to guess or have second thoughts. It just fits.

## What's Meant for You Will Always Find You

I will give you a perfect example. I love VIXA. The idea for [VIXA](https://www.vixaapp.ai/?ref=blog-how-you-do-one-thing) came to me on Easter Day. I truly feel that idea came from God. I have a lot of faith, and I believe in myself, and that idea felt like a gift.

And still, VIXA got rejected by the App Store 4 times. Yes, 4. (I wrote about [what to do when Apple rejects your app](https://lisahaven.co/blog/what-to-do-when-apple-rejects-your-app/) because I lived it.)

So I kept going. I kept fixing. That is business, and in business you push.

But at the same time, something else was happening without any push at all. I am obsessed with AI. I use it every single day to run my companies, my blog and my home. And people started noticing. 3 people came to me, naturally, asking for help. I helped them as friends. Nobody was chasing anybody.

Now I am turning that into an AI consulting business, and it makes me so happy. It is genuinely something I love to do.

That is the lesson. VIXA I fight for, because it is worth the fight. Consulting just found me. Both are mine. And the one that came easily was a sign from God that what is meant for you will always find you.

What is meant for you already has your name on it. I really believe your cards are written before you even know it.

So when something does not work out, I do not fall apart. I tell myself, "Okay, this was not meant to be." Then I pivot and keep moving forward.

## Trust That Voice in the Back of Your Mind

You know the voice I am talking about. The one in the back of your head that says:

- "Lisa, you have to do this."
- "Lisa, keep going."
- "Lisa, this is the path."
- Or sometimes: "Lisa, this is not it."

If something feels off, you just know. There are always signs. Trust that voice.

But to trust it, you have to know yourself. That is so important at some point in your life. Know your wants and your needs. Know what you will put up with and what you will not. Know your flaws to the core.

I know mine. I overdo it. I get obsessed. I run my personal life like a business sometimes. Knowing that does not make me weak. It makes me aware, and aware is where change starts.

## Watch the First 3 Seconds

This last one is really important, and it comes from real life, not a textbook. I am not a psychologist. I am a founder who has met a lot of people: happy people, jealous people, hateful people, and wonderful people.

Here is what I learned. When you share something important, a win, a loss, a big idea, try to do it in person. Then watch their face in the first 3 seconds.

That first reaction cannot be faked. People are not aware of how they respond in that moment. If you pay attention, that is your cue. That is how you know who is who.

**Here is the surprising part:** science backs this up, and the real window is even shorter than 3 seconds. Psychologist Paul Ekman spent decades studying faces. He found that [micro expressions, the quick flashes of true feeling, last a tiny fraction of a second](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3296074/). Some last as little as 1/25 of a second, which is why most people miss them.

So my 3-second rule gives you room to catch it. Watch the first flash. Watch the little jab hidden in a compliment. People run on patterns, and the pattern shows up fast.

## FAQ

### Who said "how you do one thing is how you do everything"?

Nobody knows for sure. It also goes around as "how you do anything is how you do everything." It is often linked to Zen teachings, and Martha Beck and T. Harv Eker made it popular.

### Is there a study that proves it?

There is no single study behind the quote. It is a saying people keep repeating because they see the pattern in real life: the way you handle small things often shows up in big things.

### How do you know if something is meant for you?

When it is meant for you, the energy comes back. You do not have to beg, chase or guess. You put in your best, and life gives back. That is the sign to keep going.
