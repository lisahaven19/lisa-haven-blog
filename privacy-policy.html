Privacy Policy.

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what information lisahaven.co ("this site") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information collected

  • Newsletter: when you join The Haven, your email address is collected and stored by Beehiiv, the newsletter platform that powers it.
  • Contact form: when you send a message, your name, email address, organization, and message content are collected so I can respond.
  • Technical data: like most websites, basic technical information (browser type, pages visited) may be processed by the hosting provider, Netlify, to deliver the site.

How your information is used

  • To send you the newsletter you signed up for — nothing else.
  • To respond to messages you send through the contact form.
  • To keep the site running, fast, and secure.

What is never done

  • Your information is never sold.
  • Your information is never shared with third parties for their marketing.
  • No advertising trackers are placed on this site.

Third-party services

This site relies on a small number of service providers to operate: Netlify (hosting and form processing), Beehiiv (newsletter), and Google Fonts (typography). Each processes only the data needed to provide its service, under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Every newsletter includes a one-click unsubscribe link — no questions asked.
  • You may request a copy or deletion of any personal information you have shared by sending a message through the contact page.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and no information from children is knowingly collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions? Reach out any time via the contact page.
